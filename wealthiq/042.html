What is house hacking?

The Answer

House hacking means living in part of a property while renting out another part. The rental income helps cover your housing costs while you build equity and start owning real estate.

The Fact

Owner-occupied properties can often be financed with substantially lower down payments than pure investment properties, and multi-unit properties up to four units generally still qualify for residential financing. That combination is what makes house hacking accessible.

Why This Matters

Housing is the largest expense in most budgets and produces no return. House hacking attacks that directly by turning the largest expense into a partially or fully covered one, while starting an ownership position at the same time. It is one of the few strategies that improves monthly cash flow and builds an asset simultaneously.

A Real Example

Andre buys a duplex, lives in one unit, and rents the other for $1,500. His total monthly cost, mortgage, taxes, and insurance, runs about $2,200, so his real housing cost drops to roughly $700 while he builds equity on the entire building. His coworker rents a comparable apartment for $1,600 a month and owns nothing at the end of it. Over five years the gap is significant on both sides of the ledger.

Think Like a Wealth Builder

The biggest line item in your budget is the one worth restructuring first. Housing is usually that line item.
