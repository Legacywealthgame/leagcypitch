What does ROI mean?

The Answer

ROI stands for Return on Investment. It shows how much money you made compared with how much you put in, helping you see whether an investment was worth it.

The Fact

ROI is calculated as gain divided by cost. Invest $10,000 and end with $12,000, and the ROI is 20%. It converts different opportunities of different sizes into one comparable number.

Why This Matters

Raw dollar returns are misleading without context, because a $5,000 gain is excellent on a $10,000 investment and poor on a $200,000 one. ROI is also incomplete without a time frame, since 20% over one year and 20% over ten years are wildly different outcomes described by the same percentage.

A Real Example

Terrence compares two options. One returned $8,000 on a $40,000 investment over four years, a 20% total return, roughly 4.7% annually. The other returned $6,000 on a $20,000 investment over two years, a 30% total return, roughly 14% annually. The first produced more dollars. The second was substantially the better investment, and only ROI with a time frame attached reveals that.

Think Like a Wealth Builder

Always attach a time period to ROI. A return without a timeline is not a number you can compare against anything.
