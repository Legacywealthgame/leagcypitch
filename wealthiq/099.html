Self-Employed With No Employer Benefits: Three Protections to Prioritize

The Fact

Without an employer safety net, three risks fall entirely on a self-employed person at once: an uninsured medical event, a temporary inability to earn, and zero automatic retirement contributions, each one a gap an employee would normally have partially covered.

What the Wealthy Do

The self-employed wealthy treat health coverage, income protection, and a self-directed retirement plan as non-negotiable, not optional extras to get to once the business is 'more established.'

See It Clearly

An employee has a net built in underneath them by default. The self-employed have to build and pay for their own net themselves, and skipping any strand leaves a real hole.

In the Real World

A self-employed person who becomes temporarily unable to work with no income protection in place can see both income and health coverage disappear in the same month, a risk an equivalent employee would rarely face all at once.
