What does burn rate mean in business?

The Answer

Burn rate is how quickly a business is spending its cash. If a business is burning money faster than it is bringing money in, it can run out of cash fast.

The Fact

Burn rate is typically expressed monthly, and dividing available cash by burn rate produces runway, the number of months before the business runs out of money. $90,000 in cash with a $15,000 monthly burn is six months of runway.

Why This Matters

Businesses fail from running out of cash, not from lacking profit on paper. A profitable business can fail if customers pay slowly while expenses arrive on time. Runway is the number that tells an owner how much time they have to fix something, and it is often not calculated until the situation is already urgent.

A Real Example

A design studio has $90,000 in the bank and burns about $15,000 a month, giving six months of runway. The owner assumes things are fine because two large contracts are signed, but those clients pay on 60-day terms. Payroll does not wait 60 days. Tracking runway rather than contracts is what would have shown the gap in month two instead of month five.

Think Like a Wealth Builder

Cash is the only thing a business cannot operate without for even a week. Know your runway in months, and check it monthly.
