What is the difference between being rich and being wealthy?

The Answer

Being rich can mean having a high income or a lot of money. Being wealthy means owning assets that can keep producing money over time. Wealth is about what you own, not just what you earn.

The Fact

Rich describes cash flow. Wealthy describes ownership. A high income stops the moment the work stops. Assets continue producing regardless of whether the owner is working.

Why This Matters

This distinction explains how people with extraordinary incomes end up in financial trouble. Income is a stream that requires a source to keep running. Wealth is a structure that keeps producing on its own. Chasing a higher income without converting any of it to ownership means building a larger stream with the same single point of failure.

A Real Example

A specialist physician earns $420,000 a year and spends most of it on a lifestyle sized to that income, with roughly $200,000 in assets. A retired teacher earned under $70,000 for thirty years, bought three modest rental properties, and maxed her retirement contributions. Her assets produce about $85,000 a year without her working. The doctor is rich. The teacher is wealthy, and only one of them can stop.

Think Like a Wealth Builder

Income is what you can spend. Ownership is what you can stop for. Convert one into the other deliberately or it does not happen.
