What does liquidity mean?

The Answer

Liquidity means how quickly you can turn something into cash without losing much of its value. Having liquid money helps you handle emergencies and take advantage of opportunities.

The Fact

Liquidity is a spectrum. Cash in checking is immediately available. Stocks can typically be sold within days. Real estate and business ownership often take months and involve significant transaction costs to convert.

Why This Matters

Liquidity is what determines whether you can act. It matters in both directions, covering an emergency without borrowing, and moving on an opportunity that will not wait. Being asset-rich and cash-poor is a real position, and it means being unable to act during exactly the moments that decide outcomes.

A Real Example

Miguel has roughly $600,000 in equity across two rental properties and about $3,000 in cash. When his roof fails and a tenant's furnace dies in the same month, he cannot access the $600,000 quickly. He puts $14,000 on credit cards at 24% while sitting on substantial wealth, because none of it could be reached in the window it was needed.

Think Like a Wealth Builder

Wealth you cannot reach in time is not available to you in the moment that counts. Some portion of a portfolio has to be liquid on purpose.
