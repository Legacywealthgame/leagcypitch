Wealth IQ Card 081

You just received a $20,000 inheritance. You have $8,000 of credit card debt at 22%, no investments and no savings. What should you handle first?

The Answer

Pay off the high-interest credit card debt first. Then build an emergency fund and put the remaining money toward your financial goals and investments.

The Fact

An $8,000 balance at 22% costs roughly $1,760 a year to simply carry. Paying it off is a guaranteed 22% return. No investment on earth promises 22% with certainty.

Why This Matters

There is a strong pull to put new money somewhere that feels productive. Investing feels like building. Paying off debt feels like catching up. But carrying 22% debt while chasing an 8% return means losing 14 points a year, every year, on purpose. Clearing the debt is not the boring option. It is the highest guaranteed return available to that money.

A Real Example

Devon inherits $20,000 and puts $15,000 into a brokerage account because he wants the money "working." The market gives him about 9% that year, roughly $1,350. Meanwhile his $8,000 card balance charges him about $1,760. He was up on paper, down in reality, and he never saw it because the two numbers lived on separate statements.

Think Like a Wealth Builder

Guaranteed returns come first. Uncertain returns come second. Debt at 22% is the only guaranteed 22% you will ever be offered.
