Why We Built It

A New Way for Families to Talk About Money.

Most of us grew up hearing the same advice. Work hard. Get a good job. Pay your bills.

But very few of us were taught how assets create income, how investing works, how businesses build wealth, how habits affect our money, or how to protect what we build.

As a financial planner, I have watched people learn these lessons only after real money was already on the line. It also made me realize how much I wish I had understood these things earlier in my own life.

That is why I created Legacy Wealth. To help people learn how wealth works sooner, before life teaches those lessons the expensive way.
