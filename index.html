Why We Built It

What Wealthy Families Already Know

Money becomes easier to understand when families actually talk about it.

Legacy Wealth gives families a reason to sit at the same table and make decisions around careers, income, assets, business, investing, habits, and risk together.

Designed by a financial planner, Legacy Wealth turns financial education into a competitive experience your family actually wants to play. Instead of only hearing about wealth building, players get to practice the decisions behind it before the stakes are real.

What some families learn early should not be reserved for a few.
Every family deserves the opportunity to learn how wealth works.

The Game

Play the Decisions,
Not the Dice

Pick a career. Buy assets. Pitch businesses. Duel your family over what you actually know. Every lap around the board is a year of financial decisions compressed into a few minutes.

Career Cards

Employee Self Employed Business Owner Investor

20 career cards to choose from. Each sets your salary, debt, asset limit and one edge.

Asset Cards

Investment Business Real Estate Digital Protection

62 unique asset cards across five categories. Every one has a cost and pays income on every lap.

Rich Habits & Poor Habits

Collect Pay

100 cards, and the most common space on the board.

Wealth IQ & the Financial Duel

100 Wealth IQ cards. Challenge another player head to head — answer right and you take the pot.

Pick a career

Start as an Employee, Self Employed, a Business Owner or an Investor. Each career sets your starting cash, your income per lap, your debt, how many assets you can hold, and one advantage nobody else has.

Collect income on every lap

Complete a lap and collect your salary plus the income generated by every asset you own. Earned income is where you start. Asset income is how you win.

Buy real assets

Five categories of assets, each with a cost and the income it returns every lap — from Bitcoin to a Manhattan penthouse to an AI automation business.

See What Your Habits Cost You

Rich habits can strengthen your position. Poor habits drain it. Each card connects an in-game consequence to the real-world financial principle behind it.

Weather the Downside

Market crashes. IRS audits. Financial setbacks. When the unexpected hits, your strategy gets tested. Players who built strong, durable income are better positioned to recover and keep building.

Build the Highest Legacy Value

Cash alone does not win the game. Legacy Value measures what you built and what it produces. Be the first player to reach $1 million in Legacy Value and earn the title of The Legacy Builder.

Legacy Wealth Notes

Money You Can Feel

Six denominations of Legacy Wealth Notes, from $1,000 to $100,000. Handling physical money makes every purchase, payoff, and financial decision feel more tangible.

Scan. Learn. Go Deeper.

Scan a QR code throughout the game and the AI Legacy Mentor helps explain the real-world thinking behind what you encountered, from careers and financial habits to business and wealth-building concepts.

Legacy Pitch

Turn a business idea into a real pitch. The AI Legacy Mentor helps you develop the hook, problem, solution, and value so you can present your idea at the table.

Business Blueprint

Think through what it would actually take to build the business, including what it costs, how it makes money, and what could cause it to succeed or fail.

AI Legacy Mentor

Your AI Mentor at the Table

Legacy Wealth goes beyond the board. The AI Legacy Mentor helps players explore the financial and business concepts they encounter throughout the game with personalized explanations, real-world questions, and guidance based on what they want to understand.

No complicated setup. Scan a QR code with your phone and keep learning.
