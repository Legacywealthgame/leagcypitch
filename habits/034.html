You Were Not Prepared For A Medical Emergency

The Card

A medical emergency hit your family and the bills arrived fast. Without a strong financial cushion, one crisis became a major setback. Wealth includes being prepared for what you cannot predict.

The Fact

A 2023 Federal Reserve survey found only 54% of adults had savings covering three months of expenses. A medical emergency doesn't check whether the household is ready before the bills start arriving.

Why This Matters

Medical costs are one of the few expenses that arrive with no warning and no negotiation window. Preparing for them isn't pessimism, it's simply acknowledging that "unpredictable" doesn't mean "unlikely," and building a cushion before it's needed is the only version of preparation that actually works.

A Real Example

Two families each face a sudden $18,000 medical bill in the same year. One has an emergency fund that absorbs most of it without disrupting their other goals. The other has nothing set aside, puts it on credit cards at 22%, and is still paying it off two years later, at a cost far higher than the original bill.

Think Like a Wealth Builder

An emergency fund isn't there to be used often. It's there so the one time it's needed, it doesn't turn a single bad month into a multi-year setback.
