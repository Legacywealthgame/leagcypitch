You Opened An Investment Account For Your Child

The Card

You started investing on your child's behalf early in their life. That head start gave their money decades to grow before they ever had to think about it.

The Fact

$200 a month invested from birth at an 8% average return reaches roughly $86,000 by age 18, more than double the roughly $43,200 actually deposited, with the difference coming entirely from time and compounding.

Why This Matters

A child's biggest financial advantage is time, and it's the one thing a parent can give that the child could never replicate by starting later themselves. An account opened at birth has an eighteen-year head start no account opened in early adulthood can ever fully make up.

A Real Example

A couple opens an investment account for their daughter the year she's born and contributes modestly but consistently. By the time she's an adult, the account is worth a substantial sum, built almost entirely from time rather than from any large single contribution, a head start she did nothing to earn but everything to benefit from.

Think Like a Wealth Builder

The account you open for a child today is worth more than the same account they could open for themselves in twenty years. Time given early can't be purchased back later at any price.
