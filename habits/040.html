You Failed To Protect Your Business

The Card

You never got the right insurance or legal protections in place for your business. When a claim came, you paid the full cost yourself.

The Fact

Business insurance and legal protections exist specifically because a single uncovered claim, a lawsuit, an injury on premises, a contract dispute, can cost far more than years of premiums combined, and without coverage the full amount falls entirely on the owner personally.

Why This Matters

Skipping protection saves a real but modest amount every month. The exposure it creates is open-ended, there's no cap on what an uncovered claim can cost, which makes the savings look small next to the actual risk being carried.

A Real Example

A business owner decides general liability insurance isn't worth the monthly cost. A customer is injured on the premises the following year, and the resulting claim costs several times what years of premiums would have. The savings from skipping coverage are gone in a single incident, along with considerably more.

Think Like a Wealth Builder

Insurance is priced against the odds of something happening. The owner who skips it is betting against those odds with their entire business as the stake.
