You Read 12 Business Books This Year

Collect $12,000 from the bank.

What's Really Going On

Reading is one of the cheapest forms of education available. A book that took years to write can be absorbed in hours.

The Fact

Numerous CEO reading surveys, most notably one popularized by Inc. and Business Insider, put the average CEO's reading pace at more than 60 books a year, roughly five times the pace of the average U.S. adult, who reads about 12 books annually according to Pew Research Center.

Why It Matters

Every book is someone else's expensive lessons made available for the price of a purchase. That is a return few other investments can match.

The Wealthy Move

Wealthy people treat reading as ongoing education, not a hobby reserved for vacations.
