You Never Built Savings To Fall Back On

The Card

You spent everything you earned without setting anything aside. When something unexpected happened, you had nothing to fall back on.

The Fact

A 2023 Federal Reserve survey found only 54% of adults had savings covering even three months of expenses, meaning a genuine safety net is far from universal, even among households with steady income.

Why This Matters

Having no savings doesn't just risk an emergency being harder to handle, it means every unexpected expense, however routine, becomes a genuine crisis instead of a manageable event, because there's nothing standing between the bill and real financial disruption.

A Real Example

A man earns a solid income for years but spends the full amount every month, building no cushion at all. When his car needs an unexpected $1,800 repair, he has no way to cover it except high-interest debt, turning a routine expense into a drawn-out financial setback. A colleague earning less but saving consistently covers the identical repair without a second thought.

Think Like a Wealth Builder

Income determines how much you could save. Whether you actually have anything set aside determines whether a normal, predictable expense becomes a crisis.
